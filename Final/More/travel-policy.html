Company Travel Policy

This document outlines the travel policy of the company to ensure all business travels are conducted efficiently, safely, and in compliance with company guidelines. Employees are expected to follow these rules for approval, expenses, and settlements.

Travel Types

Travel Type Definition Typical Use Cases Policy Notes
Local Travel Client visits, market supervision, internal meetings Daily travel within base city No overnight stay; DA usually not applicable
Upcountry Travel Travel outside the base city but within the country Distributor visits, branch audits, sales activation Overnight stay allowed; DA & lodging applicable
Transit Market Travel Travel covering multiple markets/cities in one trip Route sales review, multi-district market visits DA calculated per travel day; itinerary mandatory
International Travel Travel outside the country Conferences, training, supplier meetings Passport, visa, insurance & management approval required
Emergency Travel Short-notice travel due to urgent business needs Crisis handling, system failure, urgent negotiations Approval within 24 hours; justification mandatory
Training / Conference Travel Travel for learning or certification programs Sales summit, leadership training, workshops Registration fees reimbursable
Project-Based Travel Travel linked to a specific approved project New launch, system rollout, expansion activities Project code required
Client-Sponsored Travel Travel partially or fully paid by client/partner Joint programs, partner conferences Reimbursement adjusted against sponsorship
Transit / Layover Travel Travel involving extended stopovers International or remote domestic routes Hotel allowed only if layover exceeds policy hours

Travel Approval Process

All business travel must be approved by the employee's reporting manager and the finance department before booking. The process includes:

  • Submission of Travel Request Form
  • Travel itinerary including dates and locations
  • Advance request (if applicable)
  • Management approval based on necessity and policy compliance

Travel Expenses & Advances

Expenses should be claimed as per the approved travel type. Key points:

  • DA (Daily Allowance) applicable for overnight and upcountry/international travel.
  • Transport, lodging, and incidental expenses must be supported by receipts.
  • Advance requests must be justified and approved prior to travel.
  • Existing advances and overdue settlements must be checked before approving new advances.

Post-Travel Settlement

After completing travel, employees must submit a Post-Travel Settlement & Expense Report:

  • Include actual expenses incurred vs planned.
  • Attach receipts for transport, TADA, vehicle rental, guest & entertainment, and other expenses.
  • Finance department will calculate net payable/receivable amount:
    Net Settlement = Total Actual Expenses - Advance Received
  • Positive amount: company reimburses employee; negative: employee returns excess advance.

Note: All employees are expected to comply with this policy. Deviations require explicit management approval.

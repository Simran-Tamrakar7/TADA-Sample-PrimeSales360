Company Expense Policy

This document outlines the rules and guidelines for claiming expenses incurred while performing official business activities. All employees are expected to comply with these rules to ensure transparency and reimbursement compliance.

1. General Guidelines

  • All expenses must be supported by valid receipts.
  • Claims must be submitted within 30 days of incurring the expense.
  • Expenses not pre-approved by management may not be reimbursed.
  • Personal expenses cannot be claimed under any circumstances.

2. Expense Categories

Expense Type Description Limits / Notes
Transport Taxi, ride-hailing, fuel for official travel Reimbursed as per actual with receipts
TADA / Daily Allowance Daily allowance for meals and incidentals during travel Calculated based on travel type (Local / Upcountry / International)
Vehicle Rental / Fuel Rental vehicles, fuel charges for official trips Must be pre-approved; receipts mandatory
Guest & Entertainment Client meetings, hospitality expenses Max Rs X per person per day; attach invoice/receipt
Team Meeting Official team gatherings and workshops Reasonable costs only; receipts required
Other Expenses Miscellaneous approved business expenses Approval required in advance

3. Reimbursement Procedure

  • All expenses should be submitted via the company expense portal or approved forms.
  • Attach all receipts and supporting documents for verification.
  • Link expenses to the relevant Travel ID if applicable.
  • Finance team will review and approve claims within 7 business days.

4. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

  • Personal travel or meals.
  • Alcoholic beverages unless pre-approved for client hospitality.
  • Fines, penalties, or traffic violations.
  • Unauthorized gifts or donations.

5. Important Notes

Note: Any false claims or attempts to misuse the expense system may result in disciplinary action, including deduction from salary or termination.

Employees are encouraged to contact their manager or finance team for clarification regarding any unclear expenses before submitting claims.

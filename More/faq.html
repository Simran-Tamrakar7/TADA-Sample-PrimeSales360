Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How do I submit a travel request?

Go to the “My Travel Requests” page, click the + button, fill in the required details including travel type, purpose, duration, and advance if required, then click Submit.

Can I claim expenses for approved travel?

Yes, only travels that have been Approved can have linked expenses submitted. Expenses must be supported with receipts and linked to the Travel ID.

What documents are required for expense reimbursement?

All receipts and invoices must be attached. For TADA, the system calculates automatically, but receipts for transport, vehicle rental, or entertainment are mandatory.

How do I settle advances?

After your travel is completed, submit all linked expenses. The system calculates net payable/receivable automatically for advance settlement.

Who do I contact if my request is rejected?

Contact your manager or finance team directly to clarify the reason for rejection and next steps.
